About HarbourVest

An Independent, Global Private Markets Firm

Constant evolution, steadfast core

Unlocking the power of private markets

For more than 40 years, HarbourVest has been a trusted partner in private markets, investing more than $161 billion across primary, secondary, direct co-investments, infrastructure, real assets, and private credit.

We believe private markets should be accessible to all investors. Combining our global reach with local wisdom, we work closely with our clients to secure lasting futures.

Our values shape everything we do

Great people

Great outcomes begin with great people. We seek and support talented professionals who excel both in their craft and as individuals. By fostering diversity, continuous growth, and well-being, we create an environment where every unique distinct perspective strengthens our team.

Inclusive partnerships

Real collaboration depends on trust and respect. Grounded in curiosity and inclusivity, our global employees build connections with each other. We know that unique distinct perspectives and individual differences are what builds better companies and communities.

High integrity

Honesty, transparency, and strong ethics guide our every action. We treat each partner, both internal and external, with fairness and respect. We are forever mindful of the impact of our work, holding ourselves accountable for how our investments affect society, the environment, and our people.

Executional excellence

We strive for high performance, pursuing excellence in strategy and execution to drive results for our clients. Our approach is built on a commitment to continuous improvement and fostering an environment where innovative thinking can thrive every day.
